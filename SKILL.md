# CTR Optimization

Make search results more compelling by optimizing titles and meta descriptions.

## SERP-first approach (critical)
Before writing a single title option:
1. Search Google for the target query.
2. Review what the top results' titles and metas actually say.
3. Identify: common patterns, overused phrases, missing angles, weak messaging.
4. Write titles that stand out from what is already there, not in isolation.

## Output
- 3-5 title options
- 2-3 meta description options
- Brief SERP insight: what patterns you found, what differentiation angle you used

## Quality bar
Strong: clear, differentiated, intent-aligned, informed by SERP analysis.
Weak: generic, not informed by actual SERP, too similar to competitors.
