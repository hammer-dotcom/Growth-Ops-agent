# SEO Brief: "project management software for engineering teams"

## Target query
project management software for engineering teams

## Search intent
Mixed commercial/informational, mid-to-bottom funnel. The SERP splits into two intents that Google is currently treating as equally valid:
1. **Vendor solution intent** — searcher wants a specific tool built for software engineering teams (served by Asana's dedicated landing page).
2. **Comparison/listicle intent** — searcher wants a ranked shortlist across multiple tools (served by GoodDay, TheDigitalPM, Birdview PSA).

This matches Trackly's funnel focus (mid-to-bottom, comparison/decision stage) and content goal of demo requests plus AEO citations in "best project management tool" queries.

## SERP composition (top 6)
| Rank | URL | Format | Relevance to true intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | deltek.com/.../project-management | Vendor page | Low — AEC/construction engineering, not software teams |
| 2 | birdviewpsa.com listicle | Listicle | Medium — generic PM software, light software-team framing |
| 3 | celoxis.com article | Vendor/hybrid | Low — AEC/enterprise PPM, cites NASA/Tesla/Rolex, not SaaS eng teams |
| 4 | goodday.work blog | Listicle | High — direct competitor comparisons, covers Linear, ClickUp |
| 5 | asana.com/teams/engineering | Vendor page | High — only real competitor with a dedicated landing page for this exact query |
| 6 | thedigitalprojectmanager.com | Listicle | High — pricing/pros-cons matrix |

**Key insight:** Half the current top 6 (Deltek, Celoxis) are answering the wrong intent — they're AEC/construction engineering tools, not software engineering PM tools. That is weak competition sitting on a query that should belong to SaaS PM vendors. This is a gap Trackly can exploit with intent-precise content, not just better content.

## What competitors are doing

### Asana (asana.com/teams/engineering) — the only direct competitor defending this query
- Structure: hero → social proof (1M+ teams, G2 reviews) → "Why Asana" → feature deep-dives (Portfolios, Workload, Forms, Goals) → integrations (Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Slack) → analyst recognition → templates → FAQ → CTA
- Proof points: 54% faster execution, 41% more on-time delivery, 82% higher employee satisfaction, 260+ integrations, 12,000+ G2 reviews
- Named customer logos: Philips, Figma, Dell, Spotify, Indeed, Accenture — with a testimonial specifically framing Asana as bridging "technical engineering teams and business teams"
- Multiple bug-tracking, sprint-planning, and roadmap templates as CTAs, not just "start free trial"

### Listicles (GoodDay, TheDigitalPM)
- Comparison table up top, then per-tool sections with pros/cons and pricing
- Linear is consistently framed narrowly: "best for fast software issue and cycle management," praised for speed/clean UI, criticized for the 250-issue free-tier cap
- ClickUp is featured for customizability; monday.com and Notion are largely absent from the engineering-specific angle — neither listicle treats them as engineering-team tools
- Selection criteria used across articles: pricing, planning capabilities, integrations, resource management, security/scalability, admin overhead

## Gaps and differentiation opportunities for Trackly

1. **Intent-precision gap.** No listicle or vendor page filters out AEC/construction tools explicitly. A section or framing that says "if you're a software engineering team, not construction/architecture, here's what actually matters" would be unique and directly on-ICP — this doubles as an AEO answer for "best project management tool for [software] engineering teams."
2. **monday.com and Notion have no presence in this SERP as engineering-team tools.** Neither ranks nor gets meaningful coverage in the listicles for this query. Naming them by name in a comparison (where they lose on issue-tracking depth or sprint tooling) is safe — they aren't defending this ground.
3. **Linear is only described narrowly (issue/cycle speed).** No competitor content addresses Linear against broader engineering-ops needs (OKR tracking, cross-functional roadmap, async collaboration) — Trackly's own topic clusters. That's a specific, evidenced angle: "Linear is fast for issues, here's what it doesn't cover."
4. **Asana's proof points are aggregate, not engineering-specific.** Its 54%/41%/82% stats and G2 review count aren't engineering-team-specific data — Trackly can win on specificity: engineering-team-only case studies, cycle-time or sprint-completion metrics rather than generic "faster execution."
5. **No competitor content addresses OKR tracking as part of engineering PM.** It's one of Trackly's five priority clusters and is absent from every top-6 result — a clear content-gap section to own.
6. **Format:** the SERP rewards both a dedicated landing page (Asana) and a comparison listicle (GoodDay, TheDigitalPM). Given Trackly's mid/bottom funnel focus and demo-request goal, prioritize a landing page styled like Asana's but with a built-in "Trackly vs. [competitor]" comparison module — captures both intents in one asset.

## Recommended structure
1. Hero: direct value prop for software engineering teams specifically (not generic "engineering")
2. Intent-qualifier line: explicitly rule out AEC/construction use case, rule in SaaS eng teams — capture the intent-precision gap
3. Core use cases: sprint planning, issue tracking, async collaboration, OKR tracking (all five topic clusters, not just PM basics)
4. Comparison module: Trackly vs. Linear (issue speed vs. broader eng-ops), vs. Asana (engineering-specific vs. generic), vs. ClickUp/monday/Notion (purpose-built vs. general-purpose)
5. Proof: engineering-specific metrics (cycle time, sprint completion, deploy frequency correlation if available) — not generic productivity stats
6. Named customer proof, ideally logos in the 20-200 employee Series A-C range matching Trackly's ICP
7. Integrations block (Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Slack — table stakes, must match Asana's list at minimum)
8. FAQ answering "best project management software for engineering teams" directly, in a citable/AEO-friendly format
9. CTA: demo request (primary goal), not free trial-only

## CTR ideas (title/meta)
- Lead with "software engineering teams" or "SaaS engineering teams" specifically to filter out AEC search traffic and signal precision that Deltek/Celoxis lack
- Consider a "vs. Linear, Asana, ClickUp" angle in title/meta since no competitor names competitors directly in their title tags

## Gaps to close if Trackly already has a page targeting this query
- Check for: OKR tracking coverage, explicit AEC-exclusion framing, named comparisons to Linear/Asana/ClickUp/monday/Notion, engineering-specific (not generic) proof metrics, integrations parity with Asana's list (260+ claimed, Jira/GitHub/GitLab/Slack minimum)
