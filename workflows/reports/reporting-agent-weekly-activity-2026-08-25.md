## Wins
- **Two validated content gaps, confirmed across two separate query analyses.** OKR tracking appears in zero top-6 results for "project management software for engineering teams" and is absent from the engineering-specific alternatives coverage for "asana alternative for engineering teams" too. This is now a two-source-confirmed gap, not a one-off observation.
- **ClickUp self-disqualifies from the engineering-specific query.** ClickUp's own alternatives page states outright that Jira and Linear are the engineering-focused picks, positioning itself as the general/all-in-one alternative instead. That's a competitor admission Trackly content can point to directly.
- **Process discipline held under pressure.** The content-writer draft hit sections requiring real metrics and customer names (Proof, Built by teams like yours) and used explicit `[NEEDS SOURCE]` markers instead of inventing numbers or logos. The evaluator caught and confirmed all three as blocking. No fabricated claim reached the draft stage.

## Risks
- **No performance visibility at all.** Without GSC, GA4, or DataForSEO connected, there is no way to know whether Trackly's existing content is ranking, declining, or losing ground to Linear/Asana/ClickUp this week. Every finding above is about the competitive SERP landscape, not about how trackly.io itself is actually performing. This is the single biggest gap in the current setup and it compounds weekly the longer it stays off.
- **One draft is publish-blocked on real data.** The "project management software for engineering teams" draft needs real engineering-specific metrics and 2-4 ICP-matched customer references before it can ship. See the evaluation file for the full list.
- **Jira is not in the tracked competitor list but keeps appearing as a top result.** It showed up as one of the two dominant engineering-specific Asana alternatives (alongside Linear) in this week's second analysis. Competitive blind spot if left untracked. Still awaiting a decision from you on this.

## Opportunities
- **Linear owns "best Asana alternative for engineering teams" outright**, including in Google's own answer synthesis. No Trackly-tracked competitor has a dedicated page contesting this framing. Highest-priority open content slot identified this week.
- **The four Asana pain points already established in ranking content** (no native sprint object, backlog-grooming workaround, shallow Git integration depth, no CI/CD visibility) are reusable, credible framing for a Trackly vs. Asana page — the market has already agreed these are the reasons engineering teams switch.
- **Unclaimed SERP-snippet space:** no competitor currently names other competitors in their own title tags or meta descriptions for either query analyzed. Direct naming (Linear, Jira, ClickUp) in Trackly's meta descriptions is untested territory with no downside identified.

## Recommended actions

2. **Source the two blocking items** on the existing draft: real engineering-specific product metrics and 2-4 ICP-matched customer names/quotes. The draft is otherwise close to publish-ready.
3. **Decide on adding Jira** to the tracked competitor list in [site-profile.md](../../site-profile.md) — still an open question from this week's second analysis.
4. **Build the content brief and draft for "asana alternative for engineering teams"** — the competitor analysis is done and the gap is validated; this is the next in the pipeline.
5. **Consider a dedicated Trackly vs. Linear comparison page.** Linear has now shown up as the dominant, unaddressed competitor in both queries analyzed this week — it's the single most-referenced tool across both briefs.
