# Content Brief: "project management software for engineering teams"

**Source:** [workflows/seo-briefs/project-management-software-for-engineering-teams.md](../seo-briefs/project-management-software-for-engineering-teams.md)

## Decision
**Create new** — a dedicated landing page. No existing Trackly page targets this query (verify against live site before drafting; if one exists, this becomes an update, not a new page — do not create a second page competing for the same intent).

## Target query
project management software for engineering teams

## Page title (working)
Project Management Software for Software Engineering Teams | Trackly

## Search intent
Mixed commercial/informational, mid-to-bottom funnel — comparison and decision stage. Two intents currently share this SERP: buyers who want a specific vendor built for software engineering teams (Asana owns this with a dedicated page) and buyers who want a ranked shortlist across tools (served by listicles). This page should satisfy both in one asset: a vendor landing page with an embedded comparison module, so it isn't out-intended by either format.

## Content type
New page, bottom-of-funnel landing page (not a blog post). Primary conversion goal: demo request, per Trackly's content goals. Secondary goal: AEO citation in "best project management tool" and "vs" queries.

## Why this will win
1. Half the current top 6 (Deltek, Celoxis) are AEC/construction tools misranking for a software-engineering query — weak, off-intent competition sitting on real estate that should belong to a SaaS PM vendor.
2. Of Trackly's five actual competitors, only Asana defends this query directly. Linear, ClickUp, monday.com, and Notion are absent or only lightly covered in third-party listicles — open ground to name them directly in a comparison module.
3. OKR tracking, one of Trackly's five priority topic clusters, appears in zero top-6 results. No competitor addresses it as part of "engineering PM."
4. Asana's proof points (54% faster execution, 82% higher satisfaction) are generic productivity stats, not engineering-specific. Trackly can win on specificity — sprint completion, cycle time, deploy-frequency-adjacent metrics.
5. No competitor explicitly excludes AEC/construction search intent. An early qualifying line ("built for software teams, not construction or architecture firms") is unique, filters for on-ICP traffic, and doubles as a citable AEO answer.

## Key sections (in order)

1. **Hero** — value prop specific to software engineering teams (not generic "engineering"). Primary CTA: request a demo.
2. **Intent qualifier** — one or two lines ruling out AEC/construction use cases, ruling in SaaS engineering teams at 20-200 employees. Closes the intent-precision gap no competitor addresses.
3. **Core use cases** — sprint planning, issue tracking, async team collaboration, OKR tracking, engineering team productivity. All five of Trackly's priority topic clusters, not just generic PM features. OKR tracking gets its own subsection since no competitor covers it here.
4. **Comparison module** — "Trackly vs. Linear" (issue/cycle speed vs. broader eng-ops coverage), "vs. Asana" (engineering-built vs. generic-productivity-built), "vs. ClickUp / monday.com / Notion" (purpose-built vs. general-purpose, sprint/OKR-native vs. bolted-on). Direct competitor naming — safe since none of these three defend this specific query today.
5. **Proof** — engineering-specific metrics only (sprint completion rate, cycle time reduction). Avoid generic "faster execution" framing that mirrors Asana without differentiating.
6. **Customer proof** — logos/case studies from companies matching Trackly's actual ICP (Series A-C, 20-200 employees, engineering/ops leadership), not enterprise logos like Asana's Spotify/Dell/Accenture. Specificity to ICP is itself a differentiator.
7. **Integrations** — Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Slack at minimum, to match table stakes set by Asana's 260+ integrations claim. Don't need to match the count, but must not visibly lag on the tools engineering teams actually use.
8. **FAQ** — written to directly answer "best project management software for engineering teams" and comparable "vs." queries in a citable, extractable format (short, direct answers) for AEO purposes.
9. **Final CTA** — demo request, repeated. Do not lead with a free-trial-only CTA; demo requests are the stated primary content goal.

## Differentiation strategy
- Compete on intent precision (explicitly software-engineering-only) where Deltek and Celoxis are off-target.
- Compete on topic completeness (OKR tracking) where every current top-6 result is silent.
- Compete on specificity of proof (engineering-only metrics, ICP-matched customer logos) where Asana is generic.
- Name Linear, ClickUp, monday.com, and Notion directly in comparisons — none of them currently defend this query, so there is no risk of appearing defensive against an entrenched competitor narrative.

## CTR ideas (title/meta)
- Title: lead with "software engineering teams" or "SaaS engineering teams" to filter AEC-intent traffic and signal precision Deltek/Celoxis lack.
- Consider naming competitors in the meta description ("vs. Linear, Asana, ClickUp") since no competitor currently names competitors in their own title tags — this is unclaimed differentiation space in the SERP snippet itself.

## Internal linking targets
- Link to individual "Trackly vs. [Competitor]" comparison pages if/when they exist (Linear, Asana, ClickUp, monday.com, Notion) — this page should be the hub.
- Link to any existing OKR tracking, sprint planning, or issue tracking feature pages.
- Link from Trackly's homepage and any general "product" or "solutions" nav into this page as the engineering-team-specific landing spot.

## Tone and voice application
Per [tone-of-voice.md](../../tone-of-voice.md): direct, practical, evidence-led. Short sentences. Lead with proof, not promises — every claim in the Proof and Comparison sections needs a number or a named source behind it, not adjectives. Avoid: seamless, robust, game-changer, synergy, leverage (as a verb), powerful, innovative. No em dashes anywhere in the draft.

## Gaps to close if a page already exists
If Trackly already has content at this URL, audit it against: OKR tracking coverage, explicit AEC-exclusion framing, named comparisons to Linear/Asana/ClickUp/monday.com/Notion, engineering-specific (not generic) proof metrics, and integrations parity with Jira/GitHub/GitLab/Slack. Update rather than duplicate.
