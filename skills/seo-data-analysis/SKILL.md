# SEO Data Analysis

Turn SEO data into prioritized actions. The goal is interpretation and decision-making, not reporting.

## Core principles
- Use real data. Do not guess when metrics are available.
- A metric only matters if it leads to a clear action.
- Prioritize impact over activity.

## Opportunity types

**CTR opportunity**: high impressions, low CTR, already near page 1. Fix the title/meta, not the content.

**Ranking opportunity**: positions 5-20 with meaningful impressions. Content improvement can push it higher.

**Declining page**: was valuable, losing ground. Refresh or investigate.

**Content gap**: queries appearing in GSC that are only weakly addressed. May need a new or expanded page.

**Engagement mismatch**: strong search visibility but weak downstream behavior. Traffic quality or page experience issue.

## Prioritization
High priority: high impressions + positions 5-20 + strong business relevance.
Medium: moderate impressions, clear upside, fixable.
Low: very low impressions, weak relevance, major rebuild needed.

## Output
Structured table per opportunity. URL, query, metrics, opportunity type, recommended action.
