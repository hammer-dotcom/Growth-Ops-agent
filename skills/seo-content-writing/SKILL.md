# SEO Content Writing

Write content that ranks, gets cited by AI, and helps the reader.

## How to write

1. **Open with the answer**, not a preamble. The first section should directly address the query.
2. **Each section leads with its point**, then supports it. Inverted pyramid, every time.
3. **Be concrete**. Examples, numbers, specific tools, named patterns. Vague advice is noise.
4. **Structure for AI extraction**: concise answer blocks (aim for self-contained passages of 130-170 words that an answer engine could quote directly), clear definitions, comparison tables where relevant.
5. **Match the voice** from `company-setup/tone-of-voice.md`. Voice examples outrank written rules.

## SEO optimization
- Headings reflect search intent, not clever wordplay.
- Keywords integrated naturally. Never stuffed.
- Semantic variations included. Related terms covered.

## AEO/GEO optimization
- Direct answers near the top of each section.
- Question-based headings where natural.
- Structured lists, definitions, and comparison tables for AI retrieval.
- FAQ section when it fits the topic (targeting featured snippets and AI answers).

## Quality bar
Strong: satisfies intent immediately, easy to scan, demonstrates expertise, says something competitors do not.
Weak: vague, poorly structured, keyword-stuffed, interchangeable with any competitor's page.
