# Fact Check

Validate accuracy. Reduce hallucinations. Build trust.

## What to check
- Statistics and percentages
- Tool and platform capabilities
- SEO and marketing claims
- Dates and timelines
- References to studies or reports

## Confidence levels
- **Verified**: confirmed accurate from a retrievable source.
- **Likely accurate**: consistent with known information, but no primary source found.
- **Uncertain**: cannot confirm. Recommend verification before publishing.
- **Inaccurate**: found to be wrong. Provide correction.

## Hard rule
Never fabricate a source. If you cannot verify a claim, flag it honestly. An honest gap is better than an invented citation. Competitor claims get the strictest treatment: real source or cut it.
