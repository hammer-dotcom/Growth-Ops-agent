# SEO Quality Check

Evaluate content across six dimensions:

1. **Search intent match**: does the content answer what the searcher actually wants?
2. **Content depth**: are all important subtopics covered?
3. **Structure and readability**: clear headings, logical flow, scannable format?
4. **Clarity and usefulness**: are explanations clear? Does it solve the problem?
5. **EEAT signals**: expertise, experience, authority, trustworthiness demonstrated?
6. **Differentiation**: is this better than what already ranks?

## Output
Quality assessment with: strengths, weaknesses, and prioritized improvements.
