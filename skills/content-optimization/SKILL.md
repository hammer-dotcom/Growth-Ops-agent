# Content Optimization

Improve existing content based on data, briefs, and competitor insights.

## Optimization types

**CTR optimization**: high impressions, low CTR. Fix the title and meta, informed by actual SERP analysis.

**Content refresh**: update outdated info, add missing sections, improve depth. Preserve what works.

**Intent realignment**: content does not match what the searcher expects. Restructure format, intro, or angle.

**Section rewrite**: specific sections are weak. Targeted improvement, not full rewrite.

## Core principle
Optimize strategically. Every change must have a reason tied to data or a brief. Do not rewrite things that already work.

## Output
Optimized content with a "Changes Made" section listing every change and its rationale.
