# AEO / GEO Optimization

Optimize content so AI systems can retrieve, summarize, and cite it.

## Core principles

1. **AEO and GEO are not separate from SEO.** AI search systems rely on the same foundations: crawlability, indexing, relevance, authority, content quality.
2. **Answer clarity matters most.** AI systems favor content that directly answers questions in concise, self-contained passages.
3. **Non-commodity content wins.** AI systems prefer unique insights, original data, expert perspectives over generic summaries.
4. **Structure for retrieval.** Well-organized content with clear headings, definitions, and comparison tables is easier for AI to extract and quote.

## Optimization checklist
- Direct answer to the core query within the first section
- Self-contained answer blocks (130-170 words, quotable by AI)
- Clear definitions on first use of key terms
- Question-based headings where natural
- Comparison tables for "vs" or "best" topics
- EEAT signals: examples, references, expert insights, accurate claims
- FAQ section for long-tail question coverage
